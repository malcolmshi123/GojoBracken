# GojoBracken

Gojo Satoru replaces the Bracken in Lethal Company v81. He glides after you in a standing pose, and his blue aura grows stronger as the Bracken gets angrier. When the Bracken enters aggro, Gojo says “Ryōiki Tenkai: Muryōkūsho” and the music starts. The music gets louder with anger and loops until aggro ends.
