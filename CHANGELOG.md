# Changelog

## 0.3.1

- Rewrote the maintainer guides in the style of the 0.2.7 docs. Model, aura and audio behavior are unchanged.

## 0.3.0

- Refined the existing Gojo model in Blender: more symmetric posture, flat feet, a straight jacket stripe, revised hands with fingertips resting against the pants, and a continuous neck.
- Preserved the standing pose through native Unity import and retained the existing aura, Japanese voice and music.
- Rebuilt smooth surface normals for the edited clothing, face and hands to reduce harsh shading without changing the approved geometry.
- Restored a rectangular, layered jacket placket with a constant-width backing and raised central panel.
- Included the normal attachment configuration for fresh installs. Solo-test and in-ship preview modes remain disabled.
- Applied aura width/depth scale 1.2 and height scale 1.5 with a 0.8-to-1.0 flame height curve, giving calm/full flame heights of 1.2/1.5 times the authored size.
